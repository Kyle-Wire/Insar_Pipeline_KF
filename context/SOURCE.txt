Context layers in this folder: where each came from, and how current it is
===========================================================================

All are public Indiana coal-mine layers (Gibson County, Indiana). They are
in the repository because the test suite reads them, and as an example of
what a mine layer looks like. Client report areas made from them are client
data and stay on the operator's machine. For a new site, get current outlines from the operator,
the client or the state (USER_GUIDE.md, section 7) and upload them in the
builder; do not reuse these unless the site is here.

Coordinates are longitude/latitude (WGS84). Retrieval dates are the file
dates, which match the session notes. Neither publisher's terms of use were
recorded; check them before passing these files outside the operating organisation.


1. Indiana Geological & Water Survey (IGWS), ArcGIS REST services, public,
   no key. Retrieved 20-21 Sep 2026 from
       portal.igs.indiana.edu/arcgis/rest/services/
           Coal_Mines_Underground_Dec2016/FeatureServer/46
           Coal_Mines_Entries_2016/FeatureServer
           Coal_Springfield_Mines_Active_2010/FeatureServer

   coal_mines_underground_dec2016.geojson
       27 underground mine polygons, worked 1896-2015 (the layer's vintage:
       December 2016).
   coal_mine_active_gibson.geojson
       Gibson County Mine (801777), Gibson County Coal LLC, active Springfield
       seam, 25.3 km2 (the layer's vintage: 2010). This is mine 801777 only.
       A test (tests/test_fix_L4.py) uses it: its polygon has 9 holes.
   coal_mine_entries_2016.geojson
       6 shaft/slope entries (2016).

   LIMITATION. Every IGWS mine layer ends at December 2016 and the
   active-seam layers are 2010/2014. The InSAR record is 2016-2026, so these
   layers do not cover the years measured.


2. Indiana DNR Coal Mine Information System (CMIS), annual mined-area
   polygons. Supplied by Kyle as the CMIS web map
   (ArcGIS item cda8ca881758439a94e427d0b3c9c3d4); its underground-mines
   layer is
       gisdata.in.gov/server/rest/services/Hosted/CMIS_1/FeatureServer/1
   Retrieved 21 Sep 2026. Polygons carry mine_number, year_start and
   year_end (the year the area was mined). This is the authoritative source
   for which ground was mined when, through 2025.

   cmis_gibson_panels.geojson
       53 annual polygons, mined 2000-2025: mines 801777 (20), 802008 (21)
       and 802033 (12).
   cmis_all_panels_union.geojson
       Despite its name, the same 53 polygons and properties as
       cmis_gibson_panels.geojson (checked 6 Oct 2026). A config can use it as
       deramp_exclude_geojson, the ground kept out of the ramp fit.
   cmis_gibson_panels_named.geojson (made 5 Oct 2026)
       cmis_gibson_panels.geojson with a mine_name property added (the
       mines' public names; 802008 reads "Mine 802008"). Usable as a dated
       mine layer (mined_year_geojson, mined_year_field: year_start,
       mined_name_field: mine_name). Geometry unchanged.


Suggested mine_layer_sources lines (the report credits them) for a config
that uses these files. The label before the colon must be one of the
builder's layer labels (Underground mine workings, Surface mines, Other
known ground movement, Dated mined areas), or the report ignores the line.
The year the outlines are mapped to is read from the rest; a date after
"file dated", "retrieved" or "downloaded" is when the file was obtained and
is never read as that year. The dated layer above is the first:

    mine_layer_sources:
      - "Dated mined areas: Indiana Department of Natural Resources, Coal Mine Information System (CMIS) underground mines layer, annual mined-area polygons 2000-2025, file dated 2026-09-21 (when it was downloaded)"
      - "Underground mine workings: Indiana Geological & Water Survey, mines worked to 2015 (Dec 2016 layer), retrieved 20 Sep 2026"
