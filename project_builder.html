1 Project area

The ground you want numbers for. Draw it, or upload the outline. The processing box adds a margin (dashed) so there is ordinary farmland around it to compare against.

2 Mines and known movement optional, recommended

Everything here is kept out of the ramp fit (so real movement isn't removed as "atmosphere") and out of the farmland it is compared with. Keep underground and surface mines apart if you have both: the report then says whether a feature sits over workings or on surface-mine ground. Statewide layers are fine: only what lies inside the processing box is kept.

Underground workings: shapefile (.shp .dbf .shx .prj together) or .zip
Surface mines: pits, spoil, reclaimed ground
Other known movement: landfill, quarry, anything else
Dated mined areas: one polygon per panel or year, with the year it was mined in the attribute table (shapefile, GeoPackage or GeoJSON). Gives the panel-by-panel history of movement in the years after mining.

3 Extra report areas optional

Each gets its own numbers in the client report, beside "Full Satellite Imaging" and the project area. A mine can be reported too: tick "report" on it below. Give each one the plain name the client should read ("Area A1", not "area_a1_v3"): the name heads its row in the report.
